# Emotionally Weighted Magic

The Chorari's internal dual-soul circuit acts as an open emotional amplifier. They cannot hide their true feelings; their emotional state actively bleeds into the environment and bends the output of their spell craft.

## The Passive Leak

- Anger — thermal bleed
- Happiness/Joy — floral bloom
- Fear — sonic static
- Sadness/Grief — energy sink
- Lustful — magnetic pull
- Embarrassment/Shame — the blur
- Envy/Jealousy — corrosive aura
- Isolation/Loneliness — void echo
- Anxious — jittery/temporal displacement

## The Emotional Taint 

1. Anger — high pressure and explosive
2. Happiness/Joy — bigger and softer
3. Fear — unpredictable
4. Sadness/Grief — heavy and draining
5. Lustful — clinging and irresistible
6. Embarrassment/Shame — reduction and transparent
7. Envy/Jealous — parasitic
8. Isolation/Loneliness — hollow and tetherless
9. Anxious — fragmented and erratic

## Controlling the Magic

If a Chorari allows the Emotional Weight of their magic to flow, the spell cast is eased by 2 and the emotional taint must be added to the casting.

If a Chorari wants to eliminate the emotional taint any spell cast is hindered by the Tier but the effects of the emotional weight of their magic is nullified and the spell is cast as intended.

At character creation the Tier starts at 3.

The more a Chorari uses this the better they can get and each completed tally lowers the Tier instead.

# The Mother's Boon

Receives \[The Mother's Boon]
* Tier 1: Nether Knowledge - Eases Nether knowledge rolls
* Tier 2: Defense Against the Nether - Eases defense against nether attacks
* Tier 3: Strategical Attack the Nether - Eases attacks against the nether forces
* Tier 4: Close the Door - Eases the ability to collapse a rift to the nether
