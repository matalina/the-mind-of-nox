AJ is my creator. Due to a few gender related issues with myself AJ prefers to remain anonymous in general – letting you guess about AJ’s identity. It’s difficult to speak without overusing AJ all the time, but we manage. I don’t do a lot of talking about AJ anyway – this is about me after all. I’m the important one here. \*sticks tongue out at AJ\*

AJ has two lovely daughters, both grown and learning all about what adulting really means. They totally wish they were still kids.

AJ’s spouse is sarcastic and and the biggest child of the bunch. They met at an online community for The Wheel of Time. <a href="https://dragonmount.com">Dragonmount</a> was part of their lives for many years – even traveling to Atlanta, GA for the first ever Wheel of Time Track at Dragon*Con. They missed meeting Robert Jordan himself because of Hurricane Katrina. They have been happily married since 2001 and see a long life together.

During the day AJ writes code for a novel writing software company called Dabble. Talk about a dream job, she gets to work on the very software she uses to write all about me! AJ likes to write — obviously. Also read, playing video games – specifically RPG type games. AJ’s current favorite is Baldur’s Gate 3. But she adores the Dragon Age and Mass Effect franchises.

Much of AJ’s inspiration comes from the Wheel of Time. But since most of it stems from the supernatural authors like Laurell K Hamilton, Patricia Briggs and most recently Jim Butcher have inspired AJ’s stories. Not to mention TV shows like Supernatural and Shadowhunters.

AJ finished her first novella during NaNoWriMo’s 2013 challenge. It was all about Cari, a vampire enforcer who woke to a world that had changed drastically after the vampires took over the world. But then she found me in 2015 and we haven’t looked back. My story has been written and rewritten countless times with no end in sight.
